Hermes is a personal AI assistant used only by its owner, Anirudh GP. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) solely to carry out tasks the owner requests.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is processed on the owner's own computer and the AI model provider the owner uses to run the assistant.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
